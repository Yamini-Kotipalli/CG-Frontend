Safety for all.

At Rapido, the well-being of our customers is above everything else. We are constantly in pursuit of enhancing our safety measures to ensure every Rapido ride is a pleasant and comfortable experience.

Covers Everyone

For Customers

Every ride is tracked by Rapido, with access to granular latitude and longitudinal data.

For Captains

From hiring to training to monitoring ongoing checks, we take all necessary steps to ensure our Captains' safety.

Measures to ensure the well-being of both, our Captains and Customers.

Insurance

Insurance can be claimed for any accident that occurs during the ride covering OPD treatment, hospitalization, and accidental benefit with a maximum sum insured of ₹5 Lakhs. It can be claimed as soon as the ride ends.

24 x 7 Customer Support

Both, our Captains and Customers can report any kind of issues to Rapido through the 24/7 support feature on the app post the ride.

Two-way Rating System

Post the ride, both parties can give a rating to each other and any rating below 3 is flagged from Rapido's end. Rapido reaches out to them in 10 minutes to address their concern.

Way forward on Safety

To further substantiate our commitment to Safety First towards customers and captains, we will be incorporating new features on our platform very soon.
